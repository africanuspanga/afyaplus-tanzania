Pregnancy & new mothers

Pregnancy brings many questions: what to eat, what to avoid, how to cope with nausea and heartburn, and how to recover after birth. We give simple, practical guidance that works alongside your antenatal clinic, never instead of it.

Ask us about pregnancy & new mothers

We can help with

  • Food in pregnancy: folic acid, iron and a balanced plate
  • Foods, drinks and medicines to avoid
  • Nausea, heartburn and constipation
  • Healthy weight gain and staying active
  • Food for breastfeeding and recovery after birth
  • Reminders for clinic visits and tests

See a doctor quickly if

  • Any bleeding from the vagina
  • Severe headache, blurred vision, or swelling of the face and hands
  • Your baby moves less than usual
  • Fever, or water leaking before your due date

These signs need a hospital or clinic, not only advice. Please do not wait.

How a consultation works

  1. 1

    Message us

    Tell us what is worrying you, in Kiswahili or English. A short message on WhatsApp is enough to begin.

  2. 2

    We listen

    We ask a few questions about your symptoms, habits and history, so our advice fits you and not just the problem.

  3. 3

    Get a clear plan

    Food, daily habits and, where it helps, a supplement plan, explained in plain words with what to expect.

  4. 4

    We follow up

    We check in on your progress and adjust. If you need tests or treatment, we tell you clearly and point you to a hospital.
