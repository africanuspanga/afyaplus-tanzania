Is it an emergency?

If you or someone with you has severe bleeding, chest pain, difficulty breathing, a seizure, or has fainted, go to the nearest hospital straight away. Do not wait for a reply from us.

Questions people ask us

Can't see your question? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Is my conversation private?

Yes. What you tell us stays between you and the AfyaPlus team. We do not share your details, or post your story, without your permission.

Can I write in Kiswahili?

Of course. Write in Kiswahili or English, whichever is easier for you.

Do you replace my doctor or hospital?

No. We give health education, guidance and nutritional support. If your symptoms need tests or treatment, we will tell you and encourage you to visit a hospital.

Do I have to buy supplements?

No. Many concerns improve with changes to food and daily habits. If a supplement could help, we explain what it is, how to use it and what it costs before you decide.

Where are you based?

At Mlimani City, Dar es Salaam. Most consultations happen on WhatsApp or by phone, so you can reach us from anywhere in Tanzania.

How much does a consultation cost?

Send us a message on WhatsApp. We will tell you about any consultation fee and product prices before anything is agreed.
